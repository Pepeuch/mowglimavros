ArduPilot [battery arming loop](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot/master/libraries/AP_BattMonitor/AP_BattMonitor.cpp) checks every configured monitor, and its [backend check](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot/master/libraries/AP_BattMonitor/AP_BattMonitor_Backend.cpp) rejects an unhealthy monitor separately from low-voltage failsafe action. Thus `BATT_FS_LOW_ACT=0` does not solve `Battery 1 unhealthy`. Upstream [battery options](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArduPilot/ardupilot/master/libraries/AP_BattMonitor/AP_BattMonitor_Params.h) show no per-instance "ignore health for arming" bit; exact CubePilot/FCU build behavior has not been source-verified.
